# FX Full Page Screenshot – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026 (version 1.1.1)_

FX Full Page Screenshot is a browser extension that captures a full-page screenshot of the current tab and lets you edit it.

## Data collection

**FX Full Page Screenshot does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

- Screenshots are created locally in your browser and stored only on your device (in the extension's IndexedDB storage). Only the 10 most recent captures are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
- Each stored capture contains the image, the page title and the page URL, so they can be shown in the editor. This information never leaves your device.
- The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party services.
- Images leave the extension only when you choose to download or copy them.
- The optional URL and date stamp is drawn on your device from the page address and the capture time; your on/off choice for it is kept in the extension's local storage.
- The extension counts your exports in its local storage to show, once, an invitation to review it in your browser's add-on store. The count never leaves your device, and the invitation never comes back after you answer it.

## Permissions

- **activeTab**: grants temporary access to the tab you are on, only when you use the extension (toolbar icon, its right-click menu, or a keyboard shortcut), so that it can be captured.
- **scripting**: used to scroll the page during a full-page capture and restore it afterwards, or to show the area-selection overlay.
- **contextMenus**: adds the capture options to the right-click menu of the extension's toolbar icon.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored captures.
